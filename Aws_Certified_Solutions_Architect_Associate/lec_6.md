## 📖 Detailed Recap

### 1️⃣ Elasticity 📈📉

> The ability to **automatically increase or decrease resources** based on demand.

```
🔥 High demand → Provision MORE resources
😴 Low demand  → Release resources back to cloud
```

> ☁️ Cloud is **elastic** — you scale up or down whenever you want.

---

### 2️⃣ Agility 🏃

> The ability to **move fast and respond quickly** to business needs.

- ⚡ Provision infrastructure **instantly**
- 🧰 **Managed services** (coming up later!) make setting up databases & deploying apps easy
- 🤖 Cloud **automates** a lot of manual work
- 🚀 Result: Businesses release new apps/features **faster**

> 💡 **Cloud makes businesses agile.**

---

### 3️⃣ Global Reach & Geo-Distribution 🌍

| Term | Emoji | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Geo-Distribution** | 🗺️ | Deploying your app to different regions around the world |
| **Global Reach** | 🌐 | The business outcome — reaching users everywhere |

```
🖱️ Deploy to Region A, B, C...
        ⬇️
🌍 Global reach achieved
        ⬇️
🚀 Expand to new markets quickly
        ⬇️
😊 Great user experience everywhere
```

---

### 4️⃣ Availability ✅

> Is your application **available/accessible** when users need it?

> ⚠️ Recall: **Single Point of Failure (SPOF)** — if you have just **one server** and it crashes, your app becomes **unavailable**.

| Setup | Emoji | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| Single server | 1️⃣ | ❌ Low (risk of SPOF) |
| Multiple servers | 🖥️🖥️🖥️ | ✅ High |

> 💡 That's why enterprises run applications across **multiple servers** — for **high availability**.

---

### 5️⃣ Latency ⏱️

> The **time** it takes for a request to travel between the **user** and the **server**.

```
📍 Server near user  → ⚡ Low latency (fast)
📍 Server far from user → 🐢 High latency (slow)
```

> ☁️ **How Cloud Helps:** Cloud providers offer **regions all around the world**. Deploy your server in a region **closest to your users** to reduce latency.

---

### 6️⃣ Cost Management Terms 💰

#### 🏗️ CAPEX (Capital Expenditure)
> Money spent **ahead of time** — e.g., buying servers, building data centers.

#### 💳 OPEX (Operational Expenditure)
> **Variable, pay-as-you-go** expense — you pay rent based on actual usage.

```
🏗️ CAPEX (traditional)  →  💳 OPEX (cloud)
   Pay upfront                Pay as you go
   High risk                  Low risk
```

> 💡 **Moving to the cloud = trading CAPEX for OPEX.** You stop investing heavily upfront and instead pay rent only for what you use.

#### 📦 Economies of Scale
> Buying resources in **large quantities** lowers the **cost per unit**.

```
🏭 Cloud provider buys at MASSIVE scale
        ⬇️
💵 Lower cost per unit
        ⬇️
🎁 Savings passed on to YOU
```

---

### 7️⃣ Avoid Undifferentiated Heavy Lifting 🏋️

> Businesses no longer need to focus on **infrastructure management** — they can focus on **what makes them unique**.

```
☁️ Cloud handles infrastructure
        ⬇️
🎯 Business focuses on core value (e.g., product, customer experience)
```
