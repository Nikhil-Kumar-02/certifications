## 🎯 What This Lecture Is About

Time to put theory into practice! 🙌 This lecture walks through **8 real-world scenarios** and asks: *which cloud concept does this represent?*

> 💡 **How to use this doc:** Read each scenario, try to guess the concept **before** revealing the answer, then check yourself!

---

## 🎬 Scenario 1: Netflix on Friday Nights 🍿

> **Scenario:** Netflix adds **thousands of servers instantly** on Friday nights to stream to millions, then releases them back to the cloud on Monday morning.

**🔍 Focus:** Increasing servers when load is high, decreasing when it's not needed.

<details>
<summary>👉 Reveal Answer</summary>

### ✅ Elasticity 📈📉
Automatically increasing or decreasing resources based on demand.
</details>

---

## 🚀 Scenario 2: The Startup's Minimal-Cost Launch 💵

> **Scenario:** A startup launches a new app with **minimal cost** and only increases spending as user adoption grows.

**🔍 Focus:** Cost.

<details>
<summary>👉 Reveal Answer</summary>

### ✅ Pay-As-You-Go / OPEX 💳
You pay rent for the resources you use — an **Operational Expenditure**, not an upfront investment.
</details>

---

## 🏃 Scenario 3: The Product Team Moving Fast ⚡

> **Scenario:** A product team provisions infrastructure in **minutes** to test new features and release updates faster.

**🔍 Focus:** Speed.

<details>
<summary>👉 Reveal Answer</summary>

### ✅ Agility 🏃
The ability to move fast and get to market faster.
</details>

---

## 🏥 Scenario 4: The Always-On Healthcare App 🩺

> **Scenario:** A healthcare application ensures patients can access services **24×7**, even during maintenance or failures.

**🔍 Focus:** Uptime, no matter what.

<details>
<summary>👉 Reveal Answer</summary>

### ✅ Availability (High Availability) ✅
The application stays accessible even during failures or maintenance.
</details>

---

## 🌍 Scenario 5: The Global SaaS Company 🏢

> **Scenario:** A SaaS company deploys its application in **multiple regions** to serve customers worldwide with low latency.

**🔍 Focus:** Deploying across regions.

<details>
<summary>👉 Reveal Answer</summary>

### ✅ Geo-Distribution 🌍
Distributing applications across different geographies — which enables **global reach**.
</details>

---

## 🎥 Scenario 6: The Streaming Platform's Fast Playback 📺

> **Scenario:** A media streaming platform serves videos from locations **closest to the users** for faster playback.

**🔍 Focus:** Serving from nearby locations for speed.

<details>
<summary>👉 Reveal Answer</summary>

### ✅ Low Latency ⏱️
Running applications near users to minimize response/travel time.

> 💡 **Note:** This is closely related to Scenario 5 (Geo-Distribution) — geo-distribution is *how* you achieve low latency!
</details>

---

## 📦 Scenario 7: The Bulk-Buying Cloud Provider 🏭

> **Scenario:** A cloud provider buys hardware in **massive quantities** and offers low prices to customers.

**🔍 Focus:** Bulk purchasing → lower prices.

<details>
<summary>👉 Reveal Answer</summary>

### ✅ Economies of Scale 📦
Buying in huge quantities lowers the cost per unit — savings passed on to customers.
</details>

---

## 🛡️ Scenario 8: The Focused Insurance Company 🏦

> **Scenario:** An insurance company focuses on **fraud detection and customer experience**, while the cloud provider manages infrastructure.

**🔍 Focus:** Delegating infrastructure management.

<details>
<summary>👉 Reveal Answer</summary>

### ✅ Avoiding Undifferentiated Heavy Lifting 🏋️
The business delegates non-value-adding infrastructure work to the cloud provider and focuses on its core strengths.
</details>
