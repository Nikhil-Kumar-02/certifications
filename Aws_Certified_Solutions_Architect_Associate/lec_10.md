# 🔐 Lecture 10: Creating Your AWS Account — Step by Step

> 📚 **Course:** AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate
> 🧩 **Section:** Introduction to AWS
> ⏱️ **Type:** Hands-On Tutorial

---

## 🎯 What This Lecture Is About

Time to get **hands-on**! 🙌 In this lecture, we walk through the **complete AWS account creation process** — from sign-up to logging into the Management Console.

> 😌 **Set Expectations:** This process can feel a bit **tedious** (lots of verification steps), but it's a **ONE-TIME** thing. Once it's done, it's done forever!

---

## 🧾 What You'll Need Before You Start

| Requirement | Emoji | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Personal details (incl. address) | 🏠 | Identity verification |
| Valid debit/credit card | 💳 | Billing + identity verification |
| Mobile phone | 📱 | OTP/phone verification |

> ⚠️ **Why so strict?** AWS is granting you access to **very powerful infrastructure**. They need to be really sure of who you are before handing over that access.

---

## 🪜 Step-by-Step Sign-Up Process

### 1️⃣ Find the AWS Free Tier Page 🆓

> 📖 **Free Tier:** Gives you **free hands-on experience** with AWS products and services.

- 🔍 Search for **"AWS Free Tier"**
- 🖱️ Click the sponsored ad at the top, OR the **"Free Cloud Computing Services"** organic link
- ➡️ Click **"Create an account"**

---

### 2️⃣ Enter Your Root User Email 📧

> 📖 **Root User:** The main email address tied to your AWS account — the "master" identity.

- ✍️ Enter your **email address** (this becomes your root user)
- ✍️ Choose an **account name**
- 🖱️ Click **"Verify your email address"**

---

### 3️⃣ Verify Your Email ✅

- 📬 Check your inbox for a **verification code**
- 🔢 Enter the code and click **"Verify"**

| If you don't receive the code... | Action |
|---|---|
| Check spam folder | 📁 |
| Still nothing after 5–10 mins | ⏱️ Wait |
| Still nothing after that | 🔁 Click "Resend Code" |

---

### 4️⃣ Create Your Root User Password 🔑

- ✍️ Enter your **root user password**
- ✍️ Confirm it
- 🖱️ Click **"Continue"**

---

### 5️⃣ Enter Contact Information 📇

Fill in:

| Field | Emoji |
|---|---|
| Business or Personal use? | 🏢/👤 |
| Full name | 🧑 |
| Phone number | 📱 |
| Country/Region | 🌍 |
| Address, City, State, Postal Code | 🏠 |

- ☑️ Read & agree to the **Customer Agreement**
- 🖱️ Click **"Continue"**

---

### 6️⃣ Enter Billing Information 💳

- ✍️ Enter your **debit/credit card details**
- 🖱️ Click **"Verify and Continue"**

> 💡 **Good to know:** AWS won't **charge** you as long as you're within Free Tier limits. However, they may place a **temporary hold of $1** on your card just to **verify** it — this is normal and gets released.

---

### 7️⃣ Confirm Your Identity via Phone 📱

- ✍️ Enter your **mobile number**
- 🔤 Complete the **security check** (CAPTCHA-style)
- 📞 Choose **SMS** or **Voice Call** for verification
- 🖱️ Click the button to send the code

> ⏱️ **Note:** Verification code delivery can sometimes take up to **10 minutes**. If it takes longer, go back and try again.

- 🔢 Enter the verification code
- 🖱️ Click **"Continue"**

---

### 8️⃣ Select a Support Plan 🛟

AWS offers multiple support tiers:

| Plan | Cost | Recommended for Learning? |
|---|---|---|
| **Basic Support** | 🆓 Free | ✅ Yes |
| Developer/Business/Enterprise | 💰 Paid | ❌ Not needed for learning |

- 🖱️ Select **"Basic Support – Free"**
- 🖱️ Click **"Complete Sign Up"**

## 🔓 Logging Into the AWS Management Console

1. 🖱️ Click **"Go to Management Console"** (or search **"AWS Management Console"** on Google)
2. 🖱️ Click **"Sign in to Console"**
3. ✍️ Choose **"Root User"** and enter your **email address**
4. ➡️ Click **"Next"**
5. ✍️ Enter your **root user password**
6. 🖱️ Click **"Sign In"**

> 🎉 You're now inside the **AWS Management Console**!
